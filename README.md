# Angel Wings for Minecraft Java 26.3

Craft feathered wings for free flight, hovering, and charged launches. Enchant them with Agility to fly faster.

[Download the datapack](https://github.com/HeyItzAine/Angel-Wings/releases/download/v13/AngelWings.v13_mc_26.3.zip) · [Download the resource pack](https://github.com/HeyItzAine/Angel-Wings/releases/download/v13/AngelWings_RP_26.3.zip) · [Player guide](Production/Player%20guide.md)

## Flight modes

Use `/trigger aw_mode` to switch between **Angel mode** (the default) and **Elytra mode**. Elytra mode uses vanilla gliding and firework boosts. Switching during a hover restores gravity immediately. Your choice persists between sessions in this world.

## Armored wings

Drop Angel Wings and a chestplate together on an anvil to combine them. The wings keep their appearance and flight, gain the chestplate’s defenses, and use the higher level of each shared enchantment. Netherite protects the dropped wings from lava. Drop the combined pair on a grindstone to recover both items. Each gets its original enchantments back, and later upgrades stay on the wings.

## Wings are invisible when worn

If the wings appear in your hand but disappear when you equip them, try opening **Video Settings → Graphics API** and selecting **Prefer Vulkan (Experimental)**. Fully quit and reopen Minecraft for the change to take effect. In multiplayer, the player who sees invisible wings should change this setting on their own computer.
